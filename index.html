Country report · genetic diversity · complementary, model-based indicator

How much genetic diversity has each country lost globally?

Estimated from habitat loss (LUH2) and the genetic-diversity–area relationship of species with range data. A model-based estimate relevant to the genetic-diversity clause of GBF Goal A and to Target 4. How we got this.

1 · Estimated diversity remaining, % of 1850 level

Both metrics drawn; the selected one is emphasised. Line = median (q50); band = q05–q95 over z draws and species bootstrap (joint Monte Carlo at the years shown).

1b · The same series by IUCN Red List category

Selected metric and baseline; line = median (q50) of the species in each category. Category = the species' current Red List assessment (2025-2).

2a · Where the loss is, by biome

Dot = median (q50); line = q05–q95; grey bar behind = share of the country's land.

RESOLVE 2017 biomes, labelled “GET-equivalent approximation”. Strata under 5 species show no number.

2b · By taxon group

Same encoding. ▨ = confidence low.

Taxon-specific exponents are not used: the prior is one distribution per metric and marker domain.

3 · Protection: share of today's estimated diversity inside protected areas and OECMs

(APA/At)z, diversity-weighted. The 30 % line is the Target 3 area target, shown for orientation only; this bar is not a 3.1 value. Black tick = share of land area protected. A habitat-fraction transform: it does not mean diversity is maintained inside reserves — drift continues there.

4 · Demography lens (shown beside, never summed)

Population decline → diversity through the same power law. Each stratum has its own species set, n and confidence.

The area lens and this lens estimate the same latent quantity by different routes; their disagreement is a diagnostic, not an error bar.

4b · National GBF indicator assessment (observed, not modelled)

Species-by-species assessments from national demographic data in the nine-country pilot. Status today, not loss since a baseline; shown beside the modelled estimates, never summed with them.

5 · Species in this estimate

Each row is a species_country_loss record with its prov_id. Expand (▸) draws the species' own curve and the formula with its numbers substituted. Species without a Red List name show their species_id.
