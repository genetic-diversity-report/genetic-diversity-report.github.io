# Methods & provenance — run 20261024-r3 (methods_version 0.1.2)

**What the number is.** A model-based estimate of genetic diversity loss: the share of within-species genetic diversity that the species present in a country are estimated to have lost since a baseline year, inferred from habitat-area change through genetic-diversity–area relationships (GDAR). Per species the retained fraction is (A_t / A_0)^z, with A the primary habitat in the species' in-country range and z its GDAR exponent; loss is 1 minus that fraction, averaged with equal weight across species. It is a complementary, model-based indicator relevant to Goal A (genetic diversity clause) and Target 4, a candidate national or subnational indicator under decision 15/5 annex I para 1(e). Losses before 1850 are not counted: in LUH2, 10 % of the primary land present in 1700 had been converted by 1850 and 53 % by 2015 across the run's 210 countries and territories, so four fifths of the recorded conversion came after 1850; the home view recomputes these figures from the bundle, and countries that lost more than half of their 1700 primary land before 1850 carry a "conversion before 1850" badge beside their LUH2 line.

**What it is not.** It is not a substitute for headline indicator A.4; it is intended to be read alongside it and alongside A.CY.22. It uses no effective population size and no population counts, and does not measure between-population loss. It is modelled from global datasets, not national data, and has not been validated by any Party.

**Three lenses, never summed.** Area (displayed). Protection (share of today's estimated diversity inside protected areas and OECMs, 2015 habitat, WDPA September 2026). Demography (the same power law on decline from IUCN criterion-A ranges, LPI in-country trends, or the Mastretta-Yanes 2024 pilot's population counts). Area and demography are two proxies for one quantity and are never averaged. The protection share is a habitat-fraction transform, (A_PA/A_t)^z: it says how much of today's estimated diversity sits inside protected areas and OECMs, not that diversity is maintained there — genetic drift continues inside reserves, which is the point of the Ne-based indicator. Country values are not an ordering of countries: species coverage, priors and range data differ by country, so a value is read with its species count and confidence, never as a rank.

**Exponents and priors.** Two metrics (allelic richness, AC; heterozygosity / π, GD) and two marker domains, kept apart: whole-genome (Mualim 2026, 19 species; default) and microsatellite (Schmidt 2026, 48 species; sensitivity). z is modelled on the log scale, one fit per metric and domain (run 20261022-r1, reused unchanged): whole-genome AC μ −1.221, τ 0.762 (median z 0.295); whole-genome GD μ −4.017, τ 1.530 (0.018); microsatellite AC μ −2.129, τ 0.379 (0.119); microsatellite GD μ −4.700, τ 1.121 (0.0091). Between-group variance is ≈ 0 throughout; taxon-specific exponents are not used. Own exponents are used only inside their own domain (94 species × metric × domain keys in this run's loss table); every other species draws from the prior of its metric and domain — in most countries every species.

**Area basis.** Habitat area = Σ_cells species land share × country-biome land share × LUH2 primary fraction × cell area; LUH2 states exclude water, so no extra land factor applies. Polygon ranges (IUCN) enter as the fraction of each 0.25° cell they cover; plant ranges (Borgelt Maxent) as presence above the species' own threshold. Species with less than 50 km² of baseline habitat in a country, or fewer than half an effective cell, contribute no area row there.

**Displayed statistic and band.** q50, the median of 1,000 joint Monte-Carlo draws, with the 90 % band q05–q95. Each draw resamples the species (bootstrap), takes each species' own exponent draw, and applies one shared draw of the exponent prior's location to every species without its own exponent (its standard error from the mixed-model fit: whole-genome AC 0.175, GD 0.351; microsatellite AC 0.057, GD 0.151 on the log scale; 03 §5.2 amendment 2026-10-23). For the pooled unit, whose member count exceeds 50,000, the same Monte-Carlo runs in a streaming form with a per-draw bootstrap index (03 §5.2 amendment 2026-10-24). The species mean at median z (loss_point) sat outside its own band in 453,878 of 2,501,568 strata; a band must contain its centre, so q50 is shown; loss_point and loss_mean stay in the drawer (loss_mean is not shown where it is numerically meaningless, |loss_mean| > 1, which happens on some natural-habitat strata with regrown land). The band covers exponent uncertainty (between-species spread and the prior's location) and species sampling only; land-use reconstruction, protected-area and range-map error are not propagated, nor is the transfer of exponents from 19 / 48 studied species to unstudied taxa beyond that standard error.

**Coverage.** Every value shows the species count with a range map, the count with own exponent, and a confidence label (high: ≥ 100 species and ≥ 20 % of known species; medium: ≥ 20; low: otherwise, hatched). N_known is still a proxy (species whose range rasters reach the country), so the known-species share is ≈ 1 wherever a range exists and the label rests on species counts alone: "high" says the species count is large, not that the national flora and fauna are covered; small countries and islands read "low" or "medium" for the same reason. Sentences on the page give the species count without a denominator. Not available in this run: disaggregation by Red List category (planned), by indigenous and traditional territories (no data); domesticated species are not included.

**Limitations** (03-estimator §8). Exponents come from random subsampling, not clustered edge-first loss. Secondary land counts as habitat in the optimistic bound, not as recovered alleles. Microsatellite and genome-wide AC exponents differ about two-fold. Range maps are suitability or extent at 0.25–0.5°, not occupancy. The exponent is the short-term response; drift continues after loss stops (committed mid-term π column, no interval). Taxon structure in z is unsupported. Within-country self-similarity and population size proportional to area are assumptions. Land-use, protected-area and range-map errors are not propagated. IUCN criterion-A midpoints are category-level and global. Plants are the majority of most countries' species sets, so the equal-weight value sits near the plant mean; the mean of taxon-group means is stored for every stratum. Species whose in-country primary habitat is entirely gone in LUH2 count as total loss (loss 1) under the primary definition; the natural definition (incl. secondary land) is the bound shown beside it.

**Data vintages.** LUH2 v2h (850–2015, not extrapolated); Natural Earth 5.1.1; RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 ("GET-equivalent approximation"); WDPA + WD-OECM September 2026; IUCN Red List 2025-2, spatial 2022-1 (mammals, amphibians, reptiles); Borgelt 2022 plant ranges; LPI 2024; Mastretta-Yanes 2024 pilot; exponents from Schmidt 2026, Mualim 2026, Expósito-Alonso 2022. Every row carries run_id 20261024-r3, a prov_id, source DOIs and a checksum; country rows carry methods_version 0.1.2; scripts in cop17-2026/pipeline/, hashes in data-r3/90_provenance/provenance_manifest.

**Scope of this run (r3, all countries).** Every admin-0 unit with LUH2 land cells: 210 countries and territories (28 small units without LUH2 land get no estimate; disputed or unassigned land is excluded); plus all estimated countries pooled as one unit (species × country pairs, equal weight — not a global species mean). Species: every Borgelt 2022 Maxent plant with at least two presence 0.5° cells anywhere (27,198; 10 single-cell species listed, not estimated) and every terrestrial IUCN 2022-1 mammal, amphibian and reptile with a mapped range (22,432; 298 marine or freshwater-only species and 270 without a kept polygon excluded); 48,064 species enter the loss table in 186,218 species × country pairs (181,876 with area rows). Birds absent (no range maps); LUH2 ends 2015. The five focus countries (Armenia, Spain, Brazil, Kenya, United States) carry the full species tables on their pages; every other country carries its default-key tables. Earlier runs: r1 (capped, 234 species × country pairs) and r2 (the five countries, all their mapped species) stand beside the r3 strata where they exist (q50_r1); differences are the effect of species sampling and were never adjusted. Quote every value with the run id 20261024-r3.
